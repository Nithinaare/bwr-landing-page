Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At Built With Resilience, we dedicate significant coach time, individualized biomechanical programming, and weekly accountability to every client. Our refund and program pause policies are outlined below.

1. Initial 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you are dissatisfied with your initial onboarding consultation and custom plan delivery within the first 7 calendar days of your program start date, you may request a 100% refund by writing to info@builtwithresilience.com.

2. Medical & Travel Pauses

We understand that unexpected medical emergencies, surgeries, or overseas travel may arise. You may request to pause your coaching subscription for up to 60 days without forfeiting your remaining weeks.

3. Non-Refundable Items

Completed 1-on-1 personal training hours, nutrition audits that have already been finalized, and periods beyond the initial 7-day guarantee are non-refundable.
