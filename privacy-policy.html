Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Built With Resilience is committed to protecting your personal information and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, store, and utilize data when you interact with our website, request discovery consultations, or use our coaching application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide during discovery calls and onboarding: full name, email address, WhatsApp mobile number, physical metrics, fitness goals, and injury history. Technical telemetry (device type, operating system, and browser version) is logged for diagnostic stability.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used exclusively to design customized workout regimens, formulate nutrition guidance, coordinate coach communications, and provide weekly accountability check-ins. We do not sell or monetize personal data to third-party advertisers.

3. Data Security

We deploy enterprise-grade SSL encryption and secure cloud database instances to safeguard your health records, progress photos, and workout analytics.

4. Inquiries & Deletion Requests

To request a copy of your stored records or request complete deletion of your profile, contact us at info@builtwithresilience.com.
